# Signal Room launch reel — brief

Launch video for the Signal Room website, built by RiseAboveReality (www.riseabovereality.com),
posted as an Instagram collab between both accounts.

## Sources
- Live site: https://signalroom.framer.website/
- Staging: https://grounded-service-228661.framer.app

## Decisions
- Format: 16:9 only, 1920x1080, 60fps
- Length: ~30s
- Approach: hybrid — code-driven motion graphics (Remotion) from real site captures + a few AI-generated cinematic inserts
- Audio: original, generated — music bed + sound design synced to every cut (no copyright risk)
- Tone: chaotic, very fast-paced, current Instagram trends, extremely premium
- Branding: RiseAboveReality brand for the open; switch to Signal Room's own brand when it's introduced
